# PartyQueue Roadmap

PartyQueue is an open-source project with the goal of creating a truly provider-independent shared music queue.

Our vision is simple:

> **One Queue. Every Music Service.**

Instead of forcing everyone to use the same streaming platform, PartyQueue allows users to contribute music from different providers while the host keeps listening on their preferred service.

# Long-Term Vision

PartyQueue should become the easiest way for groups of friends to enjoy music together without worrying about which streaming service everyone uses.

Whether someone uses Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, Amazon Music or YouTube shouldn't matter.

The goal is a seamless shared music experience across providers while keeping the architecture modular, open and community-driven.
